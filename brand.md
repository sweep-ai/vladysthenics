# BRANDING — Vladysthenics

## 1. What This Brand Is

Vladysthenics is a weighted-calisthenics coaching brand built around one claim: skill-based strength (front lever, planche, one-arm handstand) is a *method* problem, not a *talent* problem. The brand exists to sell the method — structured volume, muscle activation, and weighted progression — to people who've been training inconsistently or randomly for years with nothing to show for it.

This mirrors the mechanism-led positioning already proven in this project's Angles framework: benefit alone ("get stronger") doesn't differentiate. The unique mechanism does.

**Working name:** Vladysthenics
**Founder:** Vlad
**Category:** Calisthenics / weighted calisthenics coaching (online, 1:1 or small group)
**Core mechanism (M-code):** `M-SYSTEM` — a structured progression built on volume management + muscle activation + weighted loading, not random bodyweight routines

## 2. Founder Story (source of truth — do not embellish beyond this)

Vlad was active growing up but never the strongest or most confident. Started calisthenics at 16. Years of slow progress taught him that consistency and proper training method mattered more than talent. At a low point in his life he found God, which gave him new purpose. Shortly after, he was forced to leave Ukraine because of the Russian invasion and rebuild his life in the United States — working to save money while restarting his training from scratch. This second attempt used efficient, structured methods: weighted calisthenics, proper volume management, muscle activation. Result: first front lever in 4 months, a 5-second one-arm handstand in 7 months. Current goal: the planche.

**Rule:** Nothing gets added to this story that Vlad hasn't said. No fabricated dialogue, no invented numbers, no dramatized scenes. If the sales letter needs more color (what the low point looked like, what leaving Ukraine looked like day-to-day, what "starting from scratch in the US" actually involved), that has to come from Vlad directly — the same standard already applied to the Jewish-fitness sales letter in this project.

**Why this story is usable as a mechanism proof, not just an emotional beat:** the timeline itself (4 months to front lever, 7 to one-arm handstand) is the proof of the method working on Vlad's own body, after a first attempt that didn't work. That's a before/after inside one person — restart with the system vs. years of unstructured effort before it. Use that contrast explicitly in copy.

| 🟦 | `#778AC0` | Glaucous | Secondary/muted — supporting UI, tags, dividers |

**Logic:** a monochrome blue system, dark-to-light. This reads as disciplined, controlled, serious — not the loud orange/red urgency palette typical of fitness offers. That's consistent with the brand's actual differentiator (method and structure over hype).

**Application rules:**
- Primary CTA buttons: Egyptian Blue (`#0E34A0`) on white, or white text on Egyptian Blue.
- Hero/dark sections: Deep Twilight (`#03045E`) or Twilight Indigo (`#2F3061`) background, white text.
- Never place Glaucous text on white — contrast is too low for body copy. Glaucous is a fill/accent color only.
- Keep the palette to these 5 values. No secondary color families (no green, no orange) — the discipline of a tight palette matches the discipline of the brand story.

## 4. Brand Voice

Grounded in Vlad's actual story, not generic fitness-marketing voice:

- **Direct, unembellished.** Short, declarative sentences. No hype adjectives ("insane," "crazy results"). Vlad's own story is dramatic enough without inflation.
- **Method over motivation.** The brand's whole argument is that talent/motivation is not the bottleneck — training structure is. Copy should reflect that: explain the *why* behind the method (volume management, muscle activation, weighted progression) rather than just promising outcomes.
- **Earned credibility, not borrowed authority.** Vlad isn't selling on credentials (no "Stanford-trained" angle available or needed). Credibility comes from the visible before/after inside his own body and his own restart.
- **Faith is present, not pushed.** The story includes his faith as a turning point. Reference it as part of the story when telling it in full (founder story sections), but don't turn every ad into a faith pitch — that risks narrowing the audience further than the offer needs to be. Let it show up as one input to his resilience, not the hook itself.
- **No fabricated testimonials.** Until real client testimonials/transcripts are collected (Fathom or otherwise), do not manufacture client quotes the way the Jewish-fitness doc used verbatim transcript lines. Mark every placeholder clearly (see sales letter).

## 5. Personas (Angle framework — Level 3/4 depth)

Built the same way persona codes are built elsewhere in this project: broad category → specific tension → moment in time.

| Code | Description | Level |
|---|---|---|
| `PLT-YEARS` | Trained calisthenics/bodyweight for years, plateaued, no visible skill progress | L3 |
| `RESTART` | Used to train, stopped, now restarting from a lower baseline and afraid of wasting time again | L3 |
| `SKILL-CHASE` | Has a specific named skill goal (front lever, planche, handstand) and has failed to progress toward it with generic programming | L3 |
| `GYM-BORED` | Comes from traditional weight training, wants a harder/more skill-based challenge, unsure where to start | L2 |

**Level 4 example (`SKILL-CHASE`):** Someone recording another failed front lever attempt on their phone, scrolling past it without posting it, wondering if their build just isn't suited for this — because nothing about their training has changed in the past year except the date.

## 7. Hook Mechanics — Fit Notes

- **Contrast** is the strongest lever here: years of unstructured training vs. 4 months with a system, inside the same person. Lead with this wherever possible.
- **Confession** works for `PLT-YEARS` and `RESTART`: admitting years of plateau or a lost restart disarms skepticism before the pitch starts.
- **Curiosity** fits `SKILL-CHASE`: "the actual reason your front lever isn't progressing" type hooks.
- **Fear** should stay light — this isn't a health-risk category. Use it only around wasted time, not body-image shame.
- **Social Proof**: not yet available. Do not use this hook until real client results/testimonials are collected. Flag as a data gap, not a copy gap.
