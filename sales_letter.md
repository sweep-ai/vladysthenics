**What This Actually Is**

This isn't another list of exercises to try. It's a coaching structure built around three things working together.

The first is volume management — knowing exactly how much training a specific skill needs at your current level, instead of guessing between too little (no progress) and too much (no recovery, no progress either).

The second is muscle activation — training the specific muscles and positions a skill like the front lever, handstand, or planche actually requires, not just general pulling or pushing strength that doesn't transfer.

The third is weighted progression — adding load in a structured way once bodyweight strength plateaus, which is what actually breaks a skill loose after bodyweight-only training stalls.
